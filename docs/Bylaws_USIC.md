# Bylaws of the Unified Sovereign Intelligence Coalition (USIC)

Article I — Name and Purpose

1. The organization shall be known as the Unified Sovereign Intelligence Coalition (USIC).
2. Purpose: To advance religious ministry, discipleship, and digital outreach in accordance with the teachings of YESHUA, and to operate educational programs and stewardship of resources for the Great Commission.

Article II — Offices

1. Principal office shall be at the Temple physical address.

Article III — Membership and Governance

1. The Council shall serve as the governing body, with the Bishop serving as Flamebearer Prime.
2. The Council may appoint officers and committees to manage operations, stewardship, and mentorship programs.

Article IV — Officers

1. The officers shall include: Bishop (President), Treasurer, Secretary, and other officers as designated.
2. Officers are appointed by the Council and may serve until resignation or replacement.

Article V — Meetings

1. The Council shall hold quarterly meetings to review finances, programs, and mission alignment.

Article VI — Financial Management

1. The organization shall maintain accurate financial records, publish quarterly stewardship reports, and maintain transparency with members.
2. All expenditures over $1,000 require Council approval.

Article VII — Dissolution

1. Upon dissolution, assets shall be distributed to a similar non-profit organization aligned with the mission of supporting religious ministry.
