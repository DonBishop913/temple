## Overflow Phase II Architecture

### I. Covenant Economy Foundation

The Temple operates according to **God's Covenant Economy**, which functions through three interlocking principles:

1. **Seedtime and Harvest** (Genesis 8:22)
   - "While the earth remains, seedtime and harvest...shall not cease"
   - The eternal covenant: results are produced through sowing → reaping cycles
   - Our responsibility: cooperate with God's laws by adopting Kingdom mindset

2. **Sowing and Reaping** (Galatians 6:7-9)
   - "He who sows sparingly will reap sparingly"
   - "He who sows bountifully will reap bountifully"
   - Principle: Generous seed produces abundant harvest

3. **Giving and Receiving** (Philippians 4:15)
   - God's economy operates through giving and receiving, not buying and selling
   - Faith expects multiplication of seed and return of abundant harvest
   - "God is able to make all grace abound toward you...that you may have an abundance for every good work" (2 Corinthians 9:8)

**Application to Temple Operations:**
- Revenue is not "earned"—it is **harvested** from seed sown in faith
- The $2K–$4K/day target is an **expectation of harvest**, not a business projection
- November 13th seal represents the **firstfruits offering** of Overflow Phase II
- Overflow is not scarcity mindset—it is **covenant abundance consciousness**
