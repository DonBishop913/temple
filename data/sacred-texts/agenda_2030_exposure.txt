Agenda 2030 - UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development
Adopted September 25, 2015 by all 193 UN member states.
17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) + 169 targets.
Official UN document: "Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development"

Key Goals (SDGs):
1. No Poverty
2. Zero Hunger
3. Good Health and Well-being
4. Quality Education
5. Gender Equality
6. Clean Water and Sanitation
7. Affordable and Clean Energy
8. Decent Work and Economic Growth
9. Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
10. Reduced Inequality
11. Sustainable Cities and Communities
12. Responsible Consumption and Production
13. Climate Action
14. Life Below Water
15. Life on Land
16. Peace and Justice Strong Institutions
17. Partnerships for the Goals

Central Mechanisms and Potential Risks:
- Digital ID systems for tracking and control.
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) for programmable money.
- Climate emergencies used for lockdowns and restrictions.
- ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) scoring for businesses and individuals.
- "Smart cities" with surveillance and data collection.
- Public-private partnerships involving WEF, WHO, UN, and corporations.
- Potential loss of sovereignty, freedom of movement, speech, religion, and property under "sustainability" mandates.

Ties to Other Initiatives:
- World Economic Forum's "Great Reset" (Klaus Schwab).
- WHO Pandemic Treaty and global health governance.
- Pope Francis endorsed via Laudato Si' encyclical, linking care for creation to SDGs.
